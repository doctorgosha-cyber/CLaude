Prepared 2026-10-07 (cloud Claude, drafts only; live site not touched). Every card bullet restates the manufacturer page in the "Source" column. No prices, no hands-on claims.

**How sources were read.** The container's network policy blocks direct fetches of manufacturer domains (petsafe.com, petcube.com, eufy.com and others returned 403 from the egress proxy). Specifications were therefore taken from (a) manufacturer-page text returned by domain-restricted web search on 2026-10-07 and (b) the AFF-4 package, whose card library (C01–C23) quotes manufacturer pages fetched directly on 2026-10-07. Amazon pages were not opened; "Amazon evidence" lists amazon.com listing titles seen in search results only. NEXUS resolves every ASIN through the Hostinger plugin and confirms the title names the same model before inserting.

Confidence: **High** = maker page or amazon.com title names the exact model; **Medium** = brand + specs match but model code or variant unconfirmed; **Low** = conflicting or unavailable listing.
