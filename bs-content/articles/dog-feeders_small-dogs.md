For a small dog the usual feeder questions flip. Hopper size barely matters when a 12 lb dog eats under a cup a day, and the numbers that decide whether a feeder works are the smallest portion it can dispense, how finely it steps up from there, whether it can slow a meal down, and whether its mechanism accepts the small kibble most small-breed and puppy formulas use. This guide applies those four filters to feeders whose makers document them, and it leans on PetSafe's own wording, "cats and small to medium-sized dogs", which our [large-dog feeder guide](/dog-feeders/large-dogs) uses as a reason to exclude the same products.

## Why Small Dogs Need a Different Checklist

A 60 lb dog eating 3 cups a day is forgiving of a feeder that rounds portions to the nearest 1/4 cup. A 9 lb dog eating 1/2 cup a day is not: a 1/8-cup error is a quarter of the meal. The same arithmetic applies to kibble shape. Small-breed and puppy formulas are often pressed into pieces under 10 mm, and some feeder mechanisms are documented for a size range rather than "any dry food". Finally, small dogs and puppies are frequently fed three or four times a day rather than two, so the meals-per-day limit and the ability to add a small "snack" slot matter more than they do for a large dog on two meals. None of this is about ruggedness, which is the large-dog problem; it is about resolution.

## How to Choose: Four Numbers to Check

- **Smallest portion and step size.** PetSafe documents "1/8 - 4 cup portions" for Simply Feed and Smart Feed. Whisker documents "1/8-cup increments" up to 1/2 cup per feeding. PETLIBRO documents a 20 ml portion (about 10 g, roughly 1/12 cup) that stacks 1 to 50 times per meal. The 5 Meal Pet Feeder has no step at all: you fill each 1-cup compartment with whatever amount you choose.
- **Meals per day.** Simply Feed and Smart Feed schedule up to 12 meals a day; Granary WiFi up to 10 "meals or snacks"; Feeder-Robot 1 to 8 times a day; the 5 Meal Pet Feeder, by definition, up to five (the first tray is available at once and four are timed).
- **Slow feed.** PetSafe's Slow Feed option "dispenses meals larger than 1/8 cup in small increments over 15 minutes" on both the Simply Feed and the Smart Feed. For a small dog that gulps, this replaces a separate slow bowl. The other three feeders here do not document an equivalent.
- **Kibble size.** PetSafe: "dry and semi-moist kibble up to a 3/4 in diameter". Whisker: "5/8 inch or less". PETLIBRO: "2–15 mm (0.08–0.6 in)". The upper limits are rarely the problem for a small dog; the lower limit on PETLIBRO's range is worth checking against very small puppy kibble, and semi-moist food is documented only by PetSafe.

Then the usual questions: power (batteries, adapter or both), cleaning, and whether an app is a feature or a dependency. Our [timed dog feeder guide](/dog-feeders/timed) explains the scheduling modes in depth, and the [smart dog feeder guide](/dog-feeders/wifi-smart) covers app control.

## App Feeders With Small Portion Steps

Whisker and PETLIBRO do not name a dog size, so their cards rest on portion documentation alone. Whisker says Feeder-Robot "works for cats or dogs", dispenses "up to 1/2 cup … in 1/8-cup increments, 1 to 8 times per day" and takes kibble of 5/8 inch or less. Its hopper (up to 32 cups) is far larger than a small dog needs, which is harmless; it simply means fewer refills. PETLIBRO's Granary WiFi is the finest-grained of the group at about 20 ml per portion, and its 5 L hopper, 2–15 mm kibble recommendation and 3 D-cell backup batteries are all on the maker's page. ## Puppies: Growth Changes the Portion Every Few Weeks

A puppy's ration climbs steadily for months, so the useful feature is not a big hopper but an easy way to nudge each meal up by one step without reprogramming the whole schedule. App feeders make that a two-tap change; button feeders like Simply Feed make it a short menu walk. Three or four meals a day is common for young puppies, which every feeder on this page supports. Two cautions: puppy kibble can be very small, so check PETLIBRO's 2 mm lower limit and run a handful through any feeder before trusting it, and no feeder replaces watching a puppy eat often enough to catch a problem. Treats add up fast at small body weights too; our [treat dispenser guide](/treat-dispensers) explains how scheduled snacks fit, and the [food calculator](/food-calculator) gives a starting daily total to discuss with your veterinarian.

## Two Small Dogs, One Feeder?

The same rule that applies to two large dogs applies here: a single bowl means the faster eater gets more. PETLIBRO's Granary line includes a dual-bowl version for two pets, and PetSafe sells Smart Feed with a 2-pet meal splitter; neither variant is carded here because their small-dog portion documentation is the same as the single-bowl models and the listings are different packages. ## Quick Checklist Before You Buy

- Weigh one of your dog's meals and confirm the feeder's smallest step divides into it
- Check the maker's pet-size wording; PetSafe states it, Whisker and PETLIBRO do not
- Choose Slow Feed (PetSafe) if your dog gulps, or plan a separate slow bowl
- Run a handful of your actual kibble through the mechanism, especially very small puppy kibble
- Count the meals per day you need now and in six months
- Decide whether you want buttons, an app, or a tray feeder with no mechanism at all

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the smallest portion an automatic dog feeder can dispense?

Among the documented models here, PETLIBRO's Granary WiFi uses a portion of about 20 ml (roughly 1/12 cup), and PetSafe's Simply Feed, Smart Feed and Whisker's Feeder-Robot step in 1/8-cup increments. A tray feeder like PetSafe's 5 Meal Pet Feeder takes any amount you place in each compartment.

### Does PetSafe say its feeders are for small dogs?

Yes. PetSafe's product pages for Healthy Pet Simply Feed, Smart Feed 2nd Generation and the 5 Meal Pet Feeder each describe them as for cats and small to medium-sized dogs.

### Can an automatic feeder slow down a small dog that eats too fast?

PetSafe documents a Slow Feed option on Simply Feed and Smart Feed that spreads meals larger than 1/8 cup over 15 minutes. The other feeders on this page do not document a slow-feed mode, so you would pair them with a slow bowl.

### Is a small-dog feeder different from a cat feeder?

Mostly not. The models here are marketed to both, and PetSafe's wording groups cats with small to medium dogs. Check the kibble size range and the pet-size wording rather than the picture on the box.
