Feeding calculator FAQ

How much should I feed my dog?

It depends on weight, age and activity. The calculator uses the NRC resting energy formula, 70 × kg0.75, and multiplies by an activity factor.

Why does the result differ from the bag label?

Bag labels are generic. They often overestimate for neutered or indoor dogs.

Can I mix wet and dry food?

Yes. Enter both products and the calculator splits the daily kcal between them.
