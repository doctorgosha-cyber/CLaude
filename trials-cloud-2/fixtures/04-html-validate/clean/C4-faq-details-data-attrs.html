Cat food FAQ

Is grain-free better for cats?

Not by itself. Cats need taurine and animal protein; grain content matters less than meat content.

How much wet food per day?

For a 4 kg indoor cat, about 200 kcal a day, which is two 85 g pouches of most complete wet foods. Use the cat calculator for your product.

Can I leave wet food out?

No longer than 30 minutes in summer, 1 hour in winter.

Does kibble clean teeth?

Only dental-specific kibble has any measurable effect. Regular kibble does not.
