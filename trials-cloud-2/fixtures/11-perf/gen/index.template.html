Best Wireless Headphones of 2026: 24 Models Compared

Six weeks, 24 pairs of headphones, 1,400 hours of listening. Here is everything we learned, with prices and ratings you can sort and filter.

How we tested

Every pair went through the same routine: a 40-track reference playlist at matched loudness, a three-hour comfort session, a commute on the number 12 tram with the noise cancelling at maximum, and a battery rundown at 50 % volume until the headphones switched themselves off. Ratings are the average of three listeners who did not know the prices.

Prices are the lowest we found from an authorised retailer on the day of publication. We update the table monthly; the comparison data is loaded live so you always see the current figures.
