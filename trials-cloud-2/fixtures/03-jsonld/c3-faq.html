Feeding FAQ

How much should I feed a 20 kg adult dog?
Feed about 250 g per day split into two meals, and adjust by body condition.
Is the kibble suitable for puppies?
No, this recipe is formulated for adult dogs; use the puppy formula under 12 months.
Does it contain grain?
The salmon recipe is grain-free; the chicken recipe contains rice.
