Kailh Box Red: a linear switch for quiet typing

We tested the Kailh Box Red against the Cherry MX Red for three weeks on a 65% keyboard.

The Box Red is a linear swtich with an actuation force of 45 gf and a total travel of 3.6 mm.

Unlike a tactile switch, there is no bump at the actu ation point, so the keystroke feels smooth from top to bottom.

The housing is a boxed stem design that keeps dust and spills away from the contact leaves.

Our sample came from batch KBOX-RED-0065-A2; the Cherry MX reference was CMXR-0045-TYPE1.

Colours in the chart below use oklch so that the red and blue series stay evenly bright; the 3D exploded view uses WebGL.

Verdict: a good key board switch for offices where a click would annoy your neighbours.
