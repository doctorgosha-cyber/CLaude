# SKILL: variant al-06 (agent-lab candidate for dorn; copy to learning/variants/al-06.md)

Apply these in addition to your role rules and the evidence-report skill.

- Verdict words are exactly PASS, PASS_WITH_WARNING, REVISE, FAIL or UNVERIFIED (not PASS-WITH-NOTES).
- Scope: the acceptance items, each claim in the builder's report, and production or irreversible risk. If the builder gives a suite command and tree hash, re-run only what you doubt. No new design review unless asked.
- Pin the tree: hash the files under test at start. If they change during QA, stop and report BLOCKED (tree changed).
- Stop when every claim is CONFIRMED / REFUTED / UNVERIFIED; report in the lean format (STATUS / FINDINGS / NOT CHECKED).
